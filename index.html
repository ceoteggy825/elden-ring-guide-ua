⏰ Deadlines — numbers on the map

Each one has its own moment — what to do, before what, and what you lose otherwise.

1
Fingerslayer Blade
Finish Seluvis (give his potion) before handing Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade — he dies afterwards.
2
Stormveil chapel
Meet him in the Stormveil chapel before killing Godrick — otherwise his quest never starts.
3
Stormhawk King
Give her the Stormhawk King before Maliketh — otherwise she leaves and never becomes Lord of Stormveil.
4
Irina
Never attack Irina (and don't use her for Varre). Her scripted death starts Hyetta; killing her yourself makes Edgar hostile and Hyetta never appears.
5
Show Latenna
Show Latenna the Right half before you pick up the Left half (Castle Sol) — otherwise she dies and you lose the Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.
6
Boggart curse
Finish Boggart's quest before Dung Eater invades in the Outer Wall moat — otherwise Boggart's curse is lost and all 5 others become mandatory.
7
Rya · Boggart
Talk to Rya (return her necklace from Boggart) before you enter Volcano Manor another way — otherwise Boggart's quest breaks and his Seedbed Curse is lost.
8
Spare him
Never kill Patches — you lose his later steps, the Dancer's Castanets for Tanith and the Crouch gesture.
9
Tragoth letter
Take Patches' Tragoth letter before killing Rykard — otherwise the Magma Whip Candlestick is lost.
10
Second Church
Reach him at the Second Church before you enter the Mountaintops — his quest locks there and Shabriri takes his body.
11
Solve before Forge!
Solve the statue riddle with Law of Regression before the Forge of the Giants (the Erdtree burns) — otherwise Age of Order is gone.

What each option leads to.

A
Seluvis's Potion
Seluvis's Potion on Nepheli ends her quest; on Dung Eater he becomes a spirit-ash puppet and his Mending Rune route closes. If you follow those quests, don't give it to them.
B
Sellen or Jerren
Gold sign = side with Sellen (her reward needs both Comet Azur and Stars of Ruin). Red sign = side with Jerren — Sellen dies. One route only.
C
Maiden blood
Use a Festering Bloody Finger on a maiden — the Chapel of Anticipation works (Nepheli's Stormhawk King is there too). Never Irina.

Three Fingers
Touching the Three Fingers overrides every other ending (Lord of Frenzied Flame) — only Miquella's Needle undoes it.
E
GOLD sign

Rykard

Ranni the Witch

Long

Longest questline. Dark Moon Greatsword + Age of the Stars ending. Drives the Blaidd, Seluvis, Sellen and Fia quests.

0 / 17 steps

Prerequisites: Caria Manor (NW Liurnia) → kill Royal Knight Loretta. If the Radahn Festival has already started, Ranni won't appear until Radahn is dead.
🔗 Linked quests
Steps 3–6 are shared with Blaidd. Finishing Ranni turns Blaidd hostile.
Step 5: Seluvis's Introduction leads to Sellen.
Step 9: Cursemark of Death is the key item for Fia's ending.
Seluvis's Potion: giving it to Nepheli ends her quest.
Seluvis's Potion on Dung Eater replaces his ending with a Spirit Ash puppet.
Rogier points you to Ranni after the Black Knifeprint.

Elden Ring Status Effects

Status Effects — How They Work

Every hit adds buildup to a separate bar for each status. When a bar fills, the status procs and the bar resets. Bars slowly drain between hits. After every proc the enemy's resistance to that status grows (5 steps) and only resets when you rest at a Site of Grace or reload the area — so in long fights procs come slower.

  • Each hit fills both bars at once, independently. Each status procs on its own when its bar is full — you don't need two weapons.
  • Example — Uchigatana +0: Keen/Standard = 45 bleed; Cold = 38 bleed + 66 frost. Cold trades some bleed and DEX scaling for frost (scales INT).
  • Real rhythm: Frost procs once per 30 s (it can't re-proc while the debuff lasts), Bleed procs as often as you fill it. So it's not Frost→Bleed→Frost→Bleed in turns — it's "Frost, then several Bleeds, then Frost again".
  • The −20% negation boosts your weapon hits. Whether it boosts the bleed proc itself is unconfirmed — bleed is %-HP and ignores defense, so most likely not.
  • Every proc raises the boss's resistance (e.g. Morgott bleed: 337 → 435 → 725 → 1182), so the cycle slows down — kill fast.
Two-status setups: Cold Uchigatana / Nagakiba (bleed + frost) · Cold or Poison Flail (bleed + frost/poison) · Cold or Poison Antspur Rapier (rot + frost/poison).

Damage Combos

Frost + Bleed
Most popular pair
Frost: 10% HP, −20% negation 30 s→Bleed ×N: 15% HP each→Frost again after 30 s
Frost opens a 30-second window where your hits deal more; inside it you proc bleed as many times as you can.
One weapon: Cold Uchigatana/Nagakiba (bleed + frost on every hit). Two: power-stance two katanas — Cold Uchigatana (L) + Keen Nagakiba (R) for more bleed per swing. ⚠ Not Rivers of Blood: it deals Fire damage, which removes Frostbite.
Frost + Fire — trade-off
Fire removes Frostbite — and lets it proc again
Frost proc: 10% HP + −20%→Fire hit removes the debuff→Frost can proc again at once
Any Fire damage instantly removes Frostbite. Minus: you lose the 30-second −20% negation window. Plus: you don't wait 30 s — the next full bar procs the 10% HP burst again (buildup isn't reset by fire).
Want the −20% window (bleed, big hits)? Keep fire away. Want repeated frost bursts? Cold weapon + a fire skill/spell after each proc. On bosses resistance grows with every proc, so the gain shrinks. FGMS is a buff, not fire damage — safe.
Scarlet Rot + Poison
Two DoTs at once
Rot: 0.18% + 15/s→Poison: 0.07% + 7/s
Different statuses run in parallel, so the DoTs add up. Good vs tanky world bosses.
Poison-infused Antspur Rapier (innate rot 55 + poison) — STR 10, DEX 20; drop from Maleigh Marais, Shaded Castle.
Frost + Rot + Bleed
Three statuses
Rotten Breath→Cold weapon→Bleed procs
Open with Rotten Breath (rot DoT runs alone), then fight with a Cold bleed weapon.
Rotten Breath: FTH 15, ARC 12 — 1 Dragon Heart at Dragon Communion (rot halved in 1.07).
Burst window
Buffs before the opener
FGMS +20%→Golden Vow +15%→Physick: Thorny Tear→Frost proc
Only one buff per category works (FGMS = body, Golden Vow = aura); tears stack freely. Buffs multiply: +20% × +15% ≈ +38%.
Thorny Cracked Tear: +9/13/20% for consecutive hits. Shard of Alexander: +15% skills.
Boss-specific picks
What to bring
Rykard, Malenia: Frost→Mohg: bleed only 7.5%→Elden Beast: no statuses
Rykard has very low frost resistance (247); Malenia frost 306 — but Freezing Pots only work twice per fight. Elden Beast is immune to all statuses and resists Holy — bring physical.
All bosses: Madness and Death Blight are useless.
