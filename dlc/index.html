Walkthrough Order — Land of Shadow

Shadow of the Erdtree quests are tightly linked: almost every NPC ends up at the Leda and Allies fight in Enir-Ilim. Two moments change everything — Miquella's charm breaking (when you approach the Shadow Keep gate) and burning the Sealing Tree, which freezes all follower quests. Every quest has a "How not to fail" block. Data checked against patch 1.17.2.

Two key moments
💔 Miquella's charm breaks

When: automatically, the first time you approach the Shadow Keep (the road north from Highroad Cross / Messmer's Army Encampment). It can fire while you are still some distance away. You hear glass shattering and see two messages: "Somewhere, a great rune has broken" and "And so too has a powerful charm".

What it does: Miquella's followers regain free will — their real quests start and they begin moving. It doesn't fail quests by itself. Before it: Ansbach's crosses, Leda's Rellana summon, Scorpion Stew for Hornsent. After it: go back and talk to everyone at their crosses.

🔥 Burning the Sealing Tree

When: only when YOU choose. You need Messmer's Kindling (drops from Messmer the Impaler, Shadow Keep). Then go to the Ancient Ruins of Rauh → Church of the Bud Main Entrance grace → defeat Romina, Saint of the Bud → interact with the Sealing Tree just past her arena.

What it does: a cutscene, then you are teleported to Enir-Ilim (final area). All follower quests freeze — the followers next appear at Enir-Ilim, as allies (Ansbach, Thiollier) or as Leda's side, depending on your choices. Before it: Freyja's letter, Moore's final choice, Grandam's Gourmet stew, Thiollier's St. Trina steps.
